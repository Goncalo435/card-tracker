Every card entered is removed from the active 416-card shoe. Clear round only clears the displayed player/dealer hands; it does not put those cards back into the shoe. New Shoe resets all 416 cards. “Opening blackjack chance” shows the chance that the next two cards from the current shoe form A + 10/J/Q/K when no player cards are entered; with one player card, it shows the chance that the next card completes a two-card blackjack. Standard blackjack rank values are used, with aces treated as 1 or 11 as appropriate.
